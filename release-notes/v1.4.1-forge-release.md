**SPT 4.1.6 · Client + Server 1.4.1 · Protocol 13** — update both the client and the server.

### New languages: Russian and Simplified Chinese

- The language buttons at the top of the enhancement window are now **한국어 · EN · RU · 中文**. They switch the window, the stat panel, and the result pop-ups at once.
- F12 → `0. Language` → `Language` adds `Russian`, `Chinese`, and `Custom`. `Auto` picks Russian on Russian, Ukrainian, or Belarusian Windows and Chinese on Chinese Windows.
- Server error messages are translated too. Only the password-protected developer options stay in English.
- Chinese uses the Windows Chinese font (Microsoft YaHei), since the bundled font has no Chinese characters.

### Translate it yourself

- `BepInEx/plugins/GearEnhance/lang/` now ships `ru.tsv`, `zh.tsv`, and a blank `template.tsv` (UTF-8, tab-separated: `ko` key · `en` reference · `text` translation). Open them in a text editor, Excel, or Google Sheets.
- Rows you edit in `ru.tsv` / `zh.tsv` override the built-in translation. Updates overwrite these two files, so keep your edits under another name.
- For a new language, copy `template.tsv` (for example to `de.tsv`), fill in the `text` column, and set F12 `Language` = `Custom` and `Custom language file` = `de.tsv`. Empty cells show English.
- The Russian and Chinese text has not had a native-speaker review yet. Corrections are very welcome.

### Stash insurance mark: smaller, movable, or off

- The 1.4.0 letter badge at the top left of every enhanced item is replaced by a **small colored corner in the lower left**, shown on **insured gear only** (gold = Premium, blue = Advanced, green = Basic).
- New F12 `1. General` settings:
  - `Stash insurance mark`: `Corner` (default) · `Letter` (the 1.4.0 badge) · `Dot` · `Name Tag` ([P] after the stars) · `Off`
  - `Stash insurance mark position`: `Bottom Left` (default) · `Top Left` (the 1.4.0 spot) · `Beside Count`
  - `Mark uninsured enhanced gear too`: off by default; on brings back the grey mark (X in the letter badge)
- Changes apply to the stash right away. For the exact 1.4.0 look, choose `Letter` + `Top Left` and turn on `Mark uninsured enhanced gear too`.

### Install / update

1. Close the server and game, and back up your profile.
2. Extract `GearEnhance-1.4.1.zip` into your SPT folder, replacing `BepInEx/plugins/GearEnhance/` (including the `fonts/` and the new `lang/` folder) and `SPT_Runtime/user/mods/GearEnhance/`.
3. Restart. All 1.4.0 records and F12 settings carry over. Protocol 13 blocks mixed client/server versions without deleting records.

### Notes

- On weapon cells the corner mark sits right next to the caliber text in the lower left. Move it with `Stash insurance mark position` or turn it off if it bothers you.
- Confirmed in game: switching all four languages, the Chinese font, the default corner mark, and the new F12 settings. The other mark shapes and positions are covered by automated tests only.
