# Gear Enhance 1.4.0 — Equipment Enhancement

## Guide {.tabset}

### PURPOSE

A client and server mod for SPT 4.1.6 that lets you enhance weapons and armor and progress through Star Evolution, Transcendence, and Mythic upgrades.

> **SPT 4.1.6 · Client + Server 1.4.0 · Protocol 12**

### INSTALLATION

1. Use **SPT 4.1.6**. Close the server and game, and back up your profile.
2. **Extract `GearEnhance-1.4.0.zip` into your SPT folder**, installing or replacing both of these directories:
   - `BepInEx/plugins/GearEnhance/` — client
   - `SPT_Runtime/user/mods/GearEnhance/` — server
3. Make sure **both the client and server are version 1.4.0**. Include the font and license files in `BepInEx/plugins/GearEnhance/fonts/`.
4. Restart the server and game. The **protocol version is 12**. If the versions do not match, the enhancement window prompts you to update both sides and prevents further actions. This warning does not erase saved records.

### HOW TO USE

1. **Right-click a weapon or armor item in your stash → Enhance gear**.
2. Choose a tab and stat, then review the **effects, success rate, cost, and consequences of failure**.
3. Select any protection options and materials you need, then make the attempt. You can also take out insurance and pay armor plate price differences in the enhancement window.

You can increase the success rate using **up to 2 types** of materials chosen from dogtags, graphics cards, and bitcoins. This does not apply to ???. For standard, Transcendence, and Mythic enhancements, you can pay an extra **₽50,000** to skip the charging animation. Success rates and failure rules remain unchanged.

### FEATURES

#### Standard Enhancement — Up to +10 per Stat

Improve **recoil control, handling, and accuracy** on weapons, and **durability and mobility** on armor.  
At +10, the default bonuses are 15% less recoil, +10 ergonomics, and 10% less bullet spread for weapons; and 30% less durability loss and 20% lower equipment penalties for armor.  
Armor plates, soft armor inserts, and visors are enhanced together with the armor they are attached to, rather than separately.

---

#### ★★ / ★★★ Star Evolution

Once every standard enhancement stat is maxed out, you can attempt **★★** evolution, followed by **★★★** evolution. Standard enhancement bonuses become **2× and 3×** as strong, respectively, while individual stat levels remain unchanged. Each effect is subject to its own cap.  
The default success rates are **30%** for ★★ and **10%** for ★★★. Failure destroys the equipment and its attachments unless you use the dedicated destruction protection ticket.

---

#### Transcendence — Further Progression After Star Evolution

Two-star Transcendence unlocks at ★★, and three-star Transcendence unlocks at ★★★. Each has **up to 5 levels, for a combined total of 10**.  
For weapons, Transcendence improves horizontal recoil and, depending on the weapon type, either fire rate or malfunction and overheating performance. For armor, it improves maximum durability, blunt damage protection, and armor class. Star multipliers do not apply.  
Failure does not destroy the equipment, but failed attempts at levels 4 and 5 lower the level by one. Downgrade protection can only be used when attempting level 4.

---

#### ??? → ✦ Mythic

With ★★★ equipment, you can spend **₽8,000,000 + 1 LEDX** to attempt **??? with a 50% success rate**. Success grants Mythic level 0, which can then be enhanced up to **level 5**. Failing ??? destroys the equipment unless you use its dedicated protection ticket.  
Mythic weapons increase the damage and penetration of fired rounds by **3% per level, up to 15%**, and reduce unfavorable ammunition stats by **16% per level, up to 80%**.  
Mythic body armor reduces damage from hits by **10% per level, up to 50%**, with half that reduction for head hits. Mythic headgear provides **5% per level, up to 25%**. Only the strongest effect among your equipped items applies.  
Mythic armor gains **up to 2** random additional protection zones, which can be rerolled. Failed Mythic level enhancement attempts do not destroy the equipment, but failures when attempting levels 3–5 lower the level by one. Downgrade protection is only available when attempting level 3.

---

#### Tabbed Interface, Ammo Comparison, and Stash Markers

Switch between Enhancement, Evolution, Transcendence, Mythic, Insurance, Prestige, and Help in a single window, with a comparison of the original and enhanced equipment on the right.  
For weapons, the **Ammo Comparison** tab compares damage, penetration, and other stats for the chambered round—or the top round in the magazine if the chamber is empty—across **Original / Current / Next Mythic Level**.  
The stash displays star and Mythic markers, progression labels such as `[Mythic V · Transcendence X]`, and Mythic borders. Gold tab notifications indicate insurance renewal or additional payments, while pink notifications indicate Prestige mail waiting to be claimed.

### INSURANCE

#### Enhancement Insurance — Protection Against Raid Losses

Take out a separate policy from trader insurance for a chance to recover equipment lost in a raid by mail, **with its enhancements, stars, Transcendence, Mythic progression, and slotted parts preserved**.  
Return chances and actual waiting times after a successful return roll are **Standard: 20% / 24 hours; Advanced: 55% / 12 hours; Premium: 99% / immediate**. The default premiums are **5% / 15% / 30%** of the enhancement value, respectively.  
If a successful enhancement or payment of the price difference for a more expensive armor plate clears your policy, you must insure the item again. When renewing or switching to a higher tier, you only pay the new premium minus the premium already paid, with a minimum charge of ₽0.

---

#### Prestige Insurance — Keep Equipment Through Your Next Prestige

Register equipment for **10% of its enhancement value** to receive it back via system mail **only when you advance to the next Prestige level**, with enhancement records, attachments, and armor plates preserved. This does not cover raid losses.  
Registration remains active through further enhancements and armor plate replacements. If the value increases, you only pay the difference. If you do not pay the difference, the equipment is returned with the enhancement records covered by your last payment.  
You must register again after the equipment is returned. Registration is unavailable at the maximum Prestige level of **6**.

### COMPATIBILITY

**Target version: SPT 4.1.6. Install both client and server version 1.4.0 (protocol 12).**

- **Mythic effects apply only to your own character, not bots.** Fika and multiplayer environments are unsupported. Mythic damage reduction does not apply to falling, bleeding, or dehydration.

- Crashes during raid gunfire have been reported when using `LootInstantSearch` and `ManimalTerminal` together. If you encounter the same issue, try removing one of them.

### SETTINGS

Press **F12** to configure the language, enhancement menu, automatic armor plate registration, sound effects and volume, reduced visual effects, Mythic stash slot shimmer, destruction confirmation dialog, and more.  
The default language setting is **Auto**: **Korean if your Windows display language is Korean, and English otherwise**. You can also select `Korean` or `English` manually. Text in the F12 settings updates after you close and reopen the settings window.

### KNOWN LIMITATIONS

- **For both weapons and armor, attempts at +6 and above for the selected stat** carry a risk of destruction or level loss on failure. Standard enhancement destruction protection and downgrade protection each prevent only their respective loss; neither increases the success rate.

- Enhancement insurance **does not guarantee a 100% return rate**. Ammunition and items inside pockets are excluded. **Trader insurance alone does not preserve enhancements.**

- **Updating from 1.3.0 preserves enhancement levels, Star Evolution, and Transcendence records.** Keep a backup, as preservation of records for new features is not guaranteed if you downgrade to an older version.

- Existing enhancement records do not carry over to equipment sold to traders or equipment whose item ID changes through selling, disassembly, reassembly, or similar actions.

- If you replace an armor plate with a more expensive one, you must pay the price difference for enhancement effects to apply. With the default settings, effects apply automatically to plates that cost the same as or less than a plate you have already paid for.
